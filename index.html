Tandem Trip Planner

Saving a reel to a map is solved. Five people saving into one shared map is not: nobody decides whose spots win. This prototype adds that decision. It pools everyone's saves, shares out the good slots fairly, and nudges the group when a saved spot is nearby.

Prototype with sample group and sample reels

Distances are straight-line estimates between real Goa locations. Ranking is a simple scoring rule in this prototype, not a trained model. Pasting a reel adds the next sample reel, since the prototype does not read Instagram.
